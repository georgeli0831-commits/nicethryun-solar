NICETHRYUN — SOLAR LIGHTING SELECTION CHECKLIST
For B2B range planning and project inquiries

1. BUYING CONTEXT
Company, destination country, buyer role and sales channel.
Product family, target quantity, sample needs and target timing.

2. THE INSTALLATION
Application: entryway, driveway, perimeter, garden or another space.
Approximate area, mounting height, material and access points.
Solar-panel exposure, shading, cable routes for split-panel products.
Site photographs or a marked plan, shared separately with the supplier.

3. OPERATING REQUIREMENTS
Nightly operating schedule and motion-sensor behavior.
Required light color, coverage and usable output.
Local seasonal daylight and temperature conditions.

4. MODEL-SPECIFIC CONFIRMATION
Ask for selected-model dimensions, mounting information and accessory list.
Confirm battery, panel, controller, runtime and test conditions together.
Match technical documents and certificates to the quoted configuration.
Do not treat a catalogue headline wattage or logo as performance evidence.

5. COMMERCIAL & ORDER DETAILS
Confirm MOQ, sample cost, pricing, lead time, packing and shipping terms.
Define private-label, instruction-language and destination labeling needs.
Agree sample approval, inspection requirements and documents before order.

This is a planning guide, not a specification, certification or quotation.
